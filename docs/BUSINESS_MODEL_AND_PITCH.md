## 1. Business Model Canvas (Executive Summary)

- **Value Proposition:** The world's first browser-native pre-departure fraud detector and post-arrival welfare guardian for migrant workers, powered by Triple-RAG and live government databases.
- **Customer Segments:**
  1. *Migrant Workers & Families (B2C):* Pre-departure contract verification, post-arrival safety monitoring.
  2. *Commercial Remittance Banks & MFIs (B2B2C):* BRAC Bank, Islami Bank Bangladesh, City Bank, bKash.
  3. *Ethical Recruiting Agencies (B2B SaaS):* BMET-licensed agencies requiring compliance audit & trust certification.
  4. *Government & Embassies (Institutional G2B):* BMET, Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare, Bangladesh foreign missions.
- **Channels:** Direct web portal, WhatsApp Business Cloud API, recruiting agency kiosks, pre-departure briefing centers.
- **Revenue Model:** High-margin hybrid monetization (Bank Bounties + B2C Micro-Payments + Agency Subscriptions).
- **Cost Structure:** Near-zero serverless infrastructure overhead (Cloudflare Pages/Workers/R2 free tier) and micro-cent LLM API costs.
