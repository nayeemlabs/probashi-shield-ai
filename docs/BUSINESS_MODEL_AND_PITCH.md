# ProbashiShield AI, Business Model, Go-to-Market & Pitch Deck Outline

## 1. Business Model Canvas (summary)

- **Customer segments:** migrants & families (B2C), ethical agencies (B2B SaaS), banks/MFIs (B2B2C partners), embassies/NGOs (institutional).
- **Value proposition:** the only pre-departure fraud detector + post-departure guardian that speaks Bangla and verifies against live government systems.
- **Channels:** WhatsApp organic + agency/union partnerships + embassy welfare programs + bank referral links.
- **Revenue streams:** micro-payments (৳299 audits), bank account-opening bounties (৳300-500), agency SaaS (৳15k-40k/mo), future government/NGO licensing.
- **Cost structure:** LLM/API costs (~৳3/audit), WhatsApp messaging, cloud infra, no physical logistics, no payroll-heavy field ops.
- **Key resources:** probashi-mcp connectors, Gulf labor-law corpus, complaint knowledge graph, embassy trust.

## 2. Unit Economics (illustrative Year-1 model, state assumptions in pitch)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| AI cost per audit | ৳2.50-3.50 |
| B2C price (full audit + audio) | ৳299 |
| Bank bounty per verified account | ৳300-500 |
| Blended revenue per paying user | > ৳300 |
| Gross margin | 98-99% |
| Break-even | Cash-flow positive from Day 1 (no burn dependency) |

Scenarios: 1 lakh paying scans → ~৳3-4 crore; 2 lakh bank openings → ~৳6-10 crore bounty; agency SaaS adds recurring layer.

## 3. Go-to-Market

1. **Pilot wedge:** partner with 2 ethical recruiting agencies + 1 labor-attaché / welfare cell → 500 onboarded migrants.
2. **Trust flywheel:** every caught fraud generates word-of-mouth + press (fraud exposés are viral in migrant communities).
3. **Institutional channel:** embassy welfare cells embed the SOS dashboard; banks co-brand "verified migrant" account-opening.
4. **Data moat:** complaint graph compounds, more users → richer sponsor history → better audits → more users.
