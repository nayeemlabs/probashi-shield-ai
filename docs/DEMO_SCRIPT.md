# ProbashiShield AI, 2-Minute Winning Demo Script

**Goal:** prove in 120 seconds that this is a working verification + response platform, not a chatbot slide.

## Act 1, Pre-Departure Audit (0:00-0:50)

1. Open the web demo (or WhatsApp sandbox view). Show a real Saudi Arabic contract + visa photo being uploaded.
2. Within ~3 seconds the audit result renders:
   - Extracted profession: **Cleaner** (broker promised "Driver")
   - Salary: **800 SAR** (promised 2,500 SAR)
   - 5 flagged hidden clauses (passport hold, exit restrictions, deductions…) with labor-law citations
   - Trust score for the agency/kafil from Graph RAG complaint history
3. Play the auto-generated **Bangla audio summary**: *"Sotorkota! Dalaler prothisruti driver howa ektu Arabic contract-e pokkho cleaner ebong beten matro 800 Riyal."*
4. Show the one-page color-coded **Fact-Based Audit Report** PDF being generated/downloaded.

**Narration beat:** *"A ChatGPT can translate. It cannot check the BMET license, the kafil's wage-theft history, or the MoFA visa registry, and it cannot speak to a semi-literate worker in Bangla. We do all four."*

## Act 2, In-Destination Lifeline (0:50-1:20)

1. Switch to the WhatsApp thread of a demo migrant ("Shafik vai").
2. Show the scheduled monthly check-in message in Bangla; play the migrant's voice-note reply; show Whisper transcript + auto-updated welfare status on the family dashboard.

## Act 3, Crisis & Rescue (1:20-2:00)

1. Trigger the demo SOS (button or voice note).
2. Show the system lock GPS coordinates, compute severity score (e.g., 87/100), and auto-generate the **Rescue Dossier** (photo, passport, kafil, location, transcript).
3. Cut to the **EOC dashboard**: live incident appears on the Middle-East map; embassy operator clicks to acknowledge; status flips to "Dispatched."

**Closing line:** *"Before the plane took off, we caught the fraud. After it landed, we never let go. ProbashiShield AI, every migrant deserves a guardian."*

## Fallbacks

- If live LLM is slow: pre-recorded screen capture + cached audit JSON as backup; still demo SOS live.
- Have printed QR codes to the report PDF and dossier for judges who want to inspect offline.
